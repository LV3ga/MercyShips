"HOLY SHIT IT'S HER!"

It is the East, and Juliet is the sun!
"I'M SO HAPPY I FOUND HER"

"COME OUT JULIET"

Who is already sick and pale with grief
That thou her maid art far more fair than she.
"I THINK YOU'RE SO BEAUTIFUL COME OUT"

Be not her maid, since she is envious.
Her vestal livery is but sick and green,
"DON'T KEEP ME WAITING"

And none but fools do wear it. Cast it off.
"COME OUT SO I MIGHT MATE WITH YOU"

It is my lady; O, it is my love!

I am too bold; 'tis not to me she speaks.
"DAMMIT I WANT HER BUT I NEED TO BE SMART"

"YOUR EYES ARE SO BEAUTIFUL AND BRIGHT THAT STARS WANT YOU TO DO THEIR JOB. I LOVE THEM SO MUCH"

"I LOVE YOUR FACE SO MUCH AGHGHGHGHHG"
